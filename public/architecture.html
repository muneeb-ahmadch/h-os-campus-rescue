A campus that adapts to changing facts.

Room disruption → updated knowledge → autonomous internal recovery → a human approves the consequential mock actions.

Approve this version

Recheck facts and permissions; atomically commit the mock booking and announcement.

Where the model matters: ordinary-language update → typed attendance/equipment proposal → human review before writing. Also: recovery explanations and role-scoped cited answers. Invalid extraction is rejected; inference failures are visible. Model output has no booking, messaging or policy authority.

One shared memory

Event, four rooms, source versions, proposal and activity history live in a session-scoped D1 world. Every source has visibility roles, version and expiry. The graph counter is the count of actual projected relationships.

Rules govern the decision

Availability · capacity · step-free access · equipment. All constraints must pass. Synthetic energy estimates rank eligible rooms. No eligible room means blocked recovery; refreshing evidence cannot fix capacity.

Approval cannot drift

A proposal binds its ID to an evidence fingerprint. Changed or expired evidence blocks execution. Compare-and-swap persists both mock effects once; old IDs and repeated approvals fail.

Three roles, filtered on the server

Organizer: review and approve. Facilities: operational sources and ingestion. Attendee: published information. Hidden sources never enter that role's prompt, graph or response.

Scope and boundaries. Synthetic/public data only; no H-FARM access, real bookings, sent messages, invoices or people monitoring. The role selector is sandbox impersonation. The agent reacts to ingestion, rather than a background scheduler. The graph is a typed structured projection. Citation checks validate references, not semantic truth. Hosted provider keys remain server-side.
